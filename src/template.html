Questions

Asked before, answered here.

Anything else, ask on WhatsApp — the person who answers is the person who runs the home.

Is there really no curfew?

Really. The flat has a biometric lock and you have your own code. Safety comes from verified housemates, a gated society and CCTV at the door, not from a gate that locks at ten.

Who will I be living with?

Working professionals and students, mostly in their twenties, co-ed. Every housemate goes through the same ID check and intro call you will, and you can meet whoever's home when you visit.

What's the minimum stay?

Three months. The agreement runs eleven months and can be renewed; after the minimum stay either side can end it with thirty days' notice.

What do I pay for beyond the rent?

Your share of electricity, LPG, and a cook or house-help if you choose to have one, all at actuals. Society move-in charges where the society asks for them. Everything else in the list above is in the rent.

Can friends or family visit?

Yes. The house agrees its own guest hours together, with a heads-up to housemates for overnight stays. Pets aren't permitted.

How do repairs and emergencies work?

Message support on working days between 10 am and 7 pm. A water leak, a power failure or anything to do with safety is answered at any hour on the emergency number you get at move-in.
